## About

Hacken Extractor is an advanced on-chain security monitoring system that provides real-time monitoring of your addresses and alerts you of any suspicious activity to prevent potential hacks.

## How it works

Hacken Extractor continuously monitors your addresses and checks every transaction against your configured triggers and detectors.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Detectors" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Detectors recognize suspicious activity and collect flagged accounts and contracts to warn against interactions with those addresses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Triggers" icon="bolt">
    A trigger is a specific condition (or set of conditions) that is monitored. When the conditions are met, the monitoring system alerts you in real time. You have full control to customise triggers to your security needs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Actions" icon="play">
    Smart Contract Actions let you automate on-chain responses when an alert fires — for example, pausing a contract or executing a withdrawal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Quick setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create your account at [extractor.hacken.dev](https://extractor.hacken.dev).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Click **Add project**, enter a name, and click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an address">
    Click **Add entity**, enter the entity name and address, choose the network, and click **Add Entity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure detectors & triggers">
    Enable the detectors you need from the **Security**, **Compliance**, or **Financial Monitoring** sections, and set up any custom triggers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also create projects and add contracts via the [REST API](/api-reference/introduction) for automated or programmatic setups.
</Tip>
