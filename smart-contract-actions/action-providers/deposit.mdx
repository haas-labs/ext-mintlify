---
title: "Deposit"
description: "How to deposit funds into an Action Provider to cover transaction gas fees."
---

In order to execute a transaction that you configured as Smart Contract Action it should have sufficient balance to cover the transaction fee. To deposit action provider:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Action Providers">
    ![Open Action Providers](/assets/create_provider_1.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a provider">
    Choose provider by clicking on it.

    ![Choose provider](/assets/choose_provider.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Deposit">
    Click on the **Deposit** button.

    ![Deposit button](/assets/deposit_1.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send funds">
    Scan the QR code or copy the deposit address to send funds.

    ![Deposit address](/assets/deposit_2.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>
