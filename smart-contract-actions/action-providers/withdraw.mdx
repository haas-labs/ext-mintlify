---
title: "Withdraw"
description: "How to withdraw funds from an Action Provider when you no longer need the Smart Contract action feature."
---

If you don't use the Smart Contract action feature anymore, you're able to withdraw all your funds from it. To do that make the following steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Action Providers">
    ![Open Action Providers](/assets/create_provider_1.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a provider">
    Choose provider by clicking on it.

    ![Choose provider](/assets/choose_provider.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Withdraw">
    Click on the **Withdraw** button.

    ![Withdraw button](/assets/withdraw_1.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter withdrawal details">
    Enter address, amount, gas tip (optionally) and click on the **Withdraw** button.

    ![Withdraw form](/assets/withdraw_2.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>
