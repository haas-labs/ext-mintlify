---
title: "Multi-Channel Notifications"
description: "Deliver Hacken Extractor alerts to Email, HTTP webhooks, Telegram, and Slack so your team is notified the moment a detector or trigger fires."
---

Hacken Extractor routes every alert to the notification channels you configure. Connect as many channels as you need and send each alert to the right people, in the tools they already use.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Send alerts to any email address. Adding a notification email does not change your account's primary email.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HTTP" icon="globe">
    Post alerts to your own HTTP endpoint (webhook) to feed SIEMs, incident tooling, or custom automation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Telegram" icon="telegram">
    Deliver alerts to a Telegram chat or group for instant mobile notifications.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Slack" icon="slack">
    Post alerts to a Slack channel so your team can triage them together.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Create a notification channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open notification settings">
    Go to **Settings** and open the **Notifications** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a channel">
    Click **Create notification channel** and choose the channel type: **Email**, **HTTP**, **Telegram**, or **Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the destination">
    Fill in the details for the selected type, for example the email address or the HTTP endpoint URL, then click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach it to your monitoring">
    When configuring a detector or trigger, select one or more channels under **Notification Channels**. Every alert it emits is delivered to all selected channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Combine channels by severity: for example, route `CRITICAL` and `HIGH` alerts to Telegram or Slack for immediate response, and send lower-severity alerts to email for review. See [Severity](/common-configurations#severity).
</Tip>
