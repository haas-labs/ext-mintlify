---
title: "Whitelisted Callers"
description: >-
  This trigger alerts you if a transaction is initiated from an address that is not on your whitelist, helping you to be informed of unauthorized access to your contract.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name">
    Enter a descriptive name for your trigger, for example: "Whitelisted Callers".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Whitelisted Entities">
    A list of addresses.
  </Step>
</Steps>
