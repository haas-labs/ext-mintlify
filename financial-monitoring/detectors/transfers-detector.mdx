title: "Transfers Detector"
---

Anomalies examples:

* Large Transfers of ERC-20 or Native tokens
* Frequent Transfers

**Detector Configuration**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name">
    Enter a descriptive name for your monitor, for example: "Transfers".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Token">
    Configure the token to monitor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track From">
    Set the source address to track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track To">
    Set the destination address to track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="From">
    Specify the from address filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="To">
    Specify the to address filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Address">
    Configure address tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Frequent">
    Enable tracking of frequent transfers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cron">
    Configure the cron schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>
